Why Rockets' 64³ Monolithic Grid Broke: Dividing a 7000u asteroid across 64 voxels produced ~109.4u cells. Precision 6.5u mining beams could only toggle entire 109u cubes, causing erratic non-spherical stepping and massive collision desync (>30u). Furthermore, re-triangulating the entire volume on the main thread blocked frames for 30–80ms.

Next-Gen Multiplayer Replication (20–30+ Players): Instead of transmitting raw voxel arrays over the network, nodes broadcast lightweight 28-byte CSG operation records: { tick, opType, x, y, z, r, mat }. Peers evaluate the analytical CSG equation locally in real time, delivering zero-bandwidth lag and 0.00u visual-to-collision parity.
